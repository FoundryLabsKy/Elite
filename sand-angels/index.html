Straight talk on price

Designer quality, honestly priced for an island.

Everything here is flown to Grand Cayman and duty is paid on it. We'd rather carry fewer, better pieces — ones you'll still be wearing in five years — than racks of throwaway swim. If a piece isn't right, an Angel will say so.

The story

Family-run, island-rooted, world-dressed.

The Dawson-James boutiques

Sand Angels is owned and run by Catherine and Ian Dawson-James. Catherine has spent more than 25 years in retail fashion in the Cayman Islands, and it shows in the buying: a designer roster you'd expect in a much bigger city, edited down to what actually works on an island.

The name isn't just a name. The stylists here are called the Angels, and the family of boutiques grew around that idea — Little Angels for kids, Activ Angels for movement, Forever Summer for everyday fashion, and Riviera next door for resort living.
