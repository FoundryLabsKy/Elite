## A0. Disambiguation — which Sand Angels?

**Audited entity:** Sand Angels (legal/trading vehicle appears as **Sand Angels Inc.**),
the swimwear & resort-wear boutique at **64 Market Street, Camana Bay, Grand Cayman,
Cayman Islands**, owned by **Catherine and Ian Dawson-James**.

How identity was confirmed: the Camana Bay landlord profile, the business's own domain
(sandangels.com), Cayman Resident, ECayOnline, FindYello and Cayman.Directory all describe
the same swim/resort boutique at Camana Bay with the same phone (345-945-7263) and email
(info@sandangels.com), and the Camana Bay press article on sister store Riviera names
"Ian and Catherine's Sand Angels Inc." as the owner of the group. This is **not** the same
business as any US/UK "Sand Angels" — every fact below is anchored to the Camana Bay store.

## A7. Market context

- **Demand cycle:** Cayman high season runs early winter→spring; record stayover arrivals
  in Feb–Apr 2026 (49,075 in Feb; best-ever March; 47,884 in April) with a typical summer
  slowdown (historic July/Sept dips) ([Cayman News Service](https://caymannewsservice.com/2026/05/tourism-boom-continues-with-record-breaking-april/), [Cayman Compass](https://www.caymancompass.com/2024/11/19/stayover-tourism-numbers-show-recent-decline/), [Tourism Analytics](https://tourismanalytics.com/caymanislands-statistics.html))
- **Customer mix:** affluent stayover tourists (Seven Mile Beach hotels/condos are minutes
  away) + resident expat/local clientele; Camana Bay is a Dart-developed town centre with
  40+ locally owned shops and strong footfall events ([Camana Bay](https://www.camanabay.com/stories/camana-bay-welcomes-newest-shop-forever-summer/))
- **Logistics/regulatory:** import-duty economy — retail prices carry Cayman import costs
  (relevant to the "prices are high" review theme); **no international shipping** offered,
  local delivery zone South Sound→West Bay; USD widely accepted alongside KYD.
