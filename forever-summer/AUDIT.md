## 7. Market Context (Cayman Islands)

- **Record tourism tailwind:** best winter season ever — **215,165 stayover visitors Dec 2025–Mar 2026**; record March 2026 (**64,213 stayover visitors**, first month over 60k); record December (54,830) and January (47,047); hotel occupancy +14% YoY in Mar 2026. New nonstop North American/Canadian airlift keeps growing (Canadian visitors +49% YoY in March).
- **Seasonality:** high season ≈ December–April (winter visitors); summer months skew local/resident + families — convenient for a brand literally named Forever Summer to run "summer never ends" counter-seasonal campaigns.
- **Customer mix:** dual audience — resident teens/young adults (repeat, weekly-drop-driven) and Seven Mile Beach tourists/cruise visitors (one-shot, discovery-driven via "shopping in Grand Cayman" searches and Camana Bay foot traffic). Camana Bay is the island's flagship Dart-owned town centre with 40+ locally owned shops, between George Town and Seven Mile Beach.
- **Import/logistics & pricing:** most Cayman goods carry 22–27% import duty, but per Cayman Resident's duty guide, **fashion/textiles are duty-free** categories — supporting the "affordable fashion" claim; residents' overseas duty-free allowance events (e.g., raised to $1,000 in December) periodically pull spend off-island, strengthening the case for "shop local, priced right" messaging. *(Duty specifics change; verify current tariff schedule before publishing claims.)*
- **Currency:** KYD (CI$) pegged at CI$1 = US$1.20; visitors think in USD — display both or clarify. **Confirm client's till currency for the site.**
- **No e-commerce regulatory barrier** identified for a local click-and-collect model; island-wide delivery is a common local courier add-on. **UNVERIFIED — confirm client's appetite/logistics.**

### 8.1 Sitemap

```
Home
├── New Arrivals            (weekly drop gallery; auto-fed from Instagram or CMS)
├── Shop                    (or "Collections" if no checkout at launch)
│   ├── Clothing (Dresses · Tops · Bottoms)
│   ├── FS Plus
│   ├── FS Girls
│   ├── Accessories
│   ├── Beauty
│   └── Gifts
├── Our Story               (Catherine & Ian, 25+ yrs, endless-summer nostalgia,
│                            the Angels family of boutiques, Catwalk for Cancer)
├── Visit Us                (map, parking, photos, hours incl. Sunday/holiday,
│                            click-to-call, WhatsApp, "next to Starbucks on the Paseo")
├── Journal / Lookbook      (optional: style edits, "what landed this week")
└── Contact                 (form + feedback routing; private complaint channel)
```

### 8.2 Key features

1. **Contact prominence:** sticky header/footer with tel link (`tel:+13459494142`), **WhatsApp click-to-chat** (dominant channel in Cayman retail — get/confirm the store's WhatsApp number), email, and hours.
2. **Weekly-drop engine:** "New this week" module on Home; Instagram feed embed (account is public-facing but login-walled for scraping — use official embed API); optional email/WhatsApp "drop alerts" signup (limited quantities → urgency is the natural CTA: "when it's gone, it's gone").
3. **Commerce, phased:** Phase 1 — browsable catalog with "Reserve via WhatsApp / call to hold"; Phase 2 — Squarespace/Shopify checkout with in-store pickup + island delivery (TABS proves local viability; Vertu's absence makes this a differentiator).
4. **Dual-audience paths:** "Visiting Grand Cayman?" block (location relative to Seven Mile Beach/cruise port, USD/KYD note) vs. resident loyalty content (drop alerts, Catwalk for Cancer).
5. **Testimonials:** Google-review strip with the "Super cute little shop… Gabby went above and beyond" quote (attributed "Google review"); post-purchase review-request QR flow.
6. **Family-of-boutiques footer:** cross-links to Sand Angels, Little Angels, Activ Angels, Riviera, Amé — shared trust and SEO equity.

### 8.3 SEO priorities

1. **Fix NAP first, then syndicate:** confirm one address string (10 Market Street #706 vs 45 Market Street) and one hours set (10–6 vs 10–7; Sunday status); publish on-site with **LocalBusiness/ClothingStore schema**.
2. **Claim/verify Google Business Profile**; align name to "Forever Summer" (not "ForeverSummer.com"), category Women's Clothing Store, load photos, enable messaging.
3. **Rename/re-geotag the Facebook page** from "ForeverSummer.com | George Town" to "Forever Summer | Camana Bay".
4. **Create the missing listings:** ecayonline, cayman.directory, findyello, Explore Cayman, Welcome to Cayman, Cayman Resident (refresh: remove "face masks"), Camana Bay directory (fix address + typo'd title if possible), Chamber of Commerce membership listing.
5. **Own the name locally:** target "Forever Summer Cayman", "affordable women's fashion Grand Cayman", "boutiques Camana Bay", "plus size clothing Cayman", "teen fashion Cayman" — and differentiate hard from the unrelated global "Forever Summer" domains (.co/.cc/.agency) with consistent "Cayman / Camana Bay" qualifiers in titles.
6. **No legacy domains to retire** (single live domain); keep foreversummer.com and 301 any old Squarespace built-in URLs if the platform changes.

### 8.4 Content/copy angles

- Lead with the verified founding emotion: *"Capture that nostalgic feeling of long summer days… dancing in the sand, falling in love, laughing with friends, feeling beautiful."*
- Standardize one tagline (recommend the Instagram line: **"Summer-inspired fashion you can afford"**).
- Credential the story: family-owned; Catherine's 25+ years dressing Cayman; five sister boutiques; Catwalk for Cancer community role.
- Merchandising honesty as urgency: hand-picked, weekly, strictly limited quantities.
- Service-reset copy on Visit Us ("our fitting rooms are yours — bring the whole haul") to counter the hostile-service review theme.
