Cayman Craft Market

Grand Cayman's official craft and souvenir market — Caymanian makers selling their own work by the sea in George Town, minutes from the cruise landing.

Administered by the Cayman Islands National Attractions Authority (CINAA).

  • Under the big tent behind Parliament, off Seafarers Way, George Town
  • Directions from the cruise landing
