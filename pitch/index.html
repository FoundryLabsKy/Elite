A website is a trust decision before it's a sales decision.

Long before a customer calls, they've already decided whether Elite feels credible. That decision happens on the site — and it happens fast.

The sales case

Fewer visitors lost. More of them turn into quotes.

Every improvement removes a reason to leave:

  • A visitor on a phone can now actually use the site, instead of giving up and calling a competitor
  • Trust signals up front mean fewer visitors who "just wanted to check reviews first" and never come back
  • A visible, one-step quote request replaces "I'll email later" — which often means never
  • Clear material and service info pre-qualifies visitors, so the calls Elite does get are more likely to close

The net effect:

  • More visitors stay long enough to see Elite's real advantages
  • More of those visitors take the one action that matters — requesting a quote
  • A site that matches the quality of the stone helps justify the price, instead of undercutting it
  • Every future improvement (real photography, a custom domain) builds on a foundation instead of patching an old one
