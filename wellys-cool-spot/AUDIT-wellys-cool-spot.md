## 7. Market context

- Cayman brands itself the "Culinary Capital of the Caribbean," with **200+ restaurants** ([VisitCaymanIslands](https://www.visitcaymanislands.com/en-us/plan-your-trip/food-drink), [Cayman.com.ky](https://www.cayman.com.ky/vacations/restaurants/)) — differentiation for a local cookshop is authenticity and price, not polish-for-polish's-sake.
- **Seasonality:** peak tourist season December–April (dry season); cruise traffic lands in George Town harbour — 2M+ cruise visitors in pre-pandemic 2019 ([FCCA via cruise guides](https://caymanislandstourbase.com/travel-tips/grand-cayman-cruise-port-(complete-guide-to-george-town))). Welly's sits on North Sound Road — a short taxi from the cruise terminal, not walkable-obvious, so the site should say "5 minutes from the cruise port" only if the owner confirms typical taxi time (`UNCONFIRMED`).
- **Customer mix:** primarily locals (reviews are explicit about this), plus adventurous visitors seeking "where the locals eat" — the site should serve both: locals need today's menu + hours + phone; visitors need the story, photos, directions, and reassurance.
- **Currency/pricing context:** KYD is pegged to USD (1 KYD ≈ 1.20 USD); island imports make food costs high, which makes Welly's value pricing a genuine differentiator worth stating carefully (without publishing unverified prices).
