The week at Welly's

Seven days, laid out like the yard game.

Dominoes run outside most afternoons — grab a seat, watch a round, or call a partner. The tiles below are the week's hours; count the pips and the halves add up to the day.

Mon 10am – 9pm
Tue 10am – 9pm
Wed 10am – 9pm
Thu 10am – 9pm cowfoot & beans day
Fri 10am – 11pm late bar night
Sat 10am – 10pm
Sun 7am – 7pm breakfast till 11am

Mon = 1 pip, Tue = 2 … Sun = 7. Doubles land Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday — same as a lucky hand.

Since Welly's day

The man who ran a tight ship.

Welly E.K. Wellington opened his cool spot on North Sound Road, and by 1982 it was already the place — locals and visitors shoulder to shoulder, drinking “greenies” (that's Heinekens, if you're new here) while the dominoes clacked outside.

The Cayman Compass remembered him as “an entrepreneur and nice guy” who ran a tight ship: anyone who was a nuisance toward his customers ended up in the parking lot. That was the whole house policy, and it worked.

Welly passed in 2009, but the spot never stopped. The pots still go on every morning, the yard game still runs, and the name over the door still means what it meant.

  1. 1982

    Already packed. Greenies flowing, dominoes outside — the cool spot earns its name.

  2. 2009

    Welly passes on. His photo lives in The People Time Forgot at the National Museum; his rules live on here.

  3. Today

    Same road, same pots, daily menu posted every morning. Come see why nothing needed changing.
